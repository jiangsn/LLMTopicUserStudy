# Visualization topic alignment study

This study examines how people interpret data visualizations. You will rate 29
statements about 10 images. It should take about **8–10 minutes**.

Participation is voluntary, and you may stop at any time by closing the browser.
Responses are linked to an anonymous study identifier and, when applicable, your
Prolific participant ID. Do not enter identifying information in free-text fields.

Risks are no greater than ordinary computer use. You may not benefit directly, but
the findings may improve our understanding of human and machine descriptions of
visualization complexity.
