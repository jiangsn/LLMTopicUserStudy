# Visualization topic alignment study

> **APPROVAL REQUIRED — NOT FOR RECRUITMENT.** Replace this notice only with the
> participation language confirmed by Prof. Jian Chen and the applicable Ohio State
> review process.

This study examines how people interpret data visualizations. You will rate 29
statements about 10 images. It is expected to take about **8–10 minutes**.

Participants who complete the study through Prolific will receive **$2.00**. The study
records your Prolific participant, study, and session identifiers; demographic answers;
ratings; optional free-text answers; response timing; viewport information; and study
navigation events. Study data are stored in a secured Firebase project controlled by the
research team. Do not enter names, email addresses, or other identifying information in
free-text fields.

Participation is voluntary. You may stop before submission by closing the browser.
